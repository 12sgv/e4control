Management is seeking input on a work policy that will affect your five-member team. Management values employee input and allows employees to vote on the policy they prefer. Each team member’s vote will count equally, and the policy that receives the majority of votes will determine the policy for your team.

You will vote for one of two policies under consideration, fully remote or hybrid. These policies are described below:

  • Fully remote policy: Work remotely 5 full days per week.
  • Hybrid policy: Work in-office 3 full days per week and remotely the remaining 2 full days.

The policy with the most votes within your team will become your team's policy. If your preferred policy wins the vote, you would receive benefits related to being able to work under your preferred policy. However, if your preferred policy loses the vote, you would incur costs related to not being able to work under your preferred policy.

Please vote for the policy you prefer.
