Management is seeking input on a work policy that will be implemented for your five-member team next month. Management values employee input and allows employees to vote for the policy they prefer. Each team member’s vote will count equally, and the policy that receives more team member votes will be implemented for that team. In the rare event that a team member does not vote and the vote ends in a tie, the missing vote will be randomly assigned to determine the winning policy.

You will vote for one of two policies under consideration: fully remote or fully in-office. Under the fully remote work policy, all members of your team will be required to work remotely. Under the fully in-office work policy, all members of your team will be required to be in the office during normal work hours.

The policy with the most votes within your team will be implemented for your team. Please vote for the policy you prefer.
