Principal Investigator: Spencer Vogrinec

Institution: University of Pittsburgh

Email: spencer.vogrinec@pitt.edu

Purpose of the study: The purpose of this study is to examine how people make decisions. If you agree to take part in this research, you will assume the role of an employee. In this role, you will be asked to make several decisions and answer some follow-up questions. This study involves minimal risk to you.

Possible discomforts and risks: We do not anticipate any psychological discomfort or risks of any form during this study. There should be no risk of injury from participating in this study. This study does not involve any deception in any form. Your participation is strictly voluntary, and there are no right or wrong answers to the judgments and decisions you will make.

Benefits to you and others: This study can help accounting and management professionals understand how individuals make decisions.

Compensation: You will be paid a fixed participation fee of $1.50 for completing the study. Additionally, you may have the opportunity to receive a bonus depending on your decisions and the decision of others during the study. Participating in the study will cost you only your time, which is estimated to be approximately 5-15 minutes.

Privacy and confidentiality: Your responses will be held in confidence. The results, if presented, will be presented in summary form only.

If you do not want to take part in the study: Participation is entirely voluntary. You are free to refuse to participate in the study and your refusal will not influence your current or future relationships with the University of Pittsburgh. If you decide not to complete the study, please return the submission so another individual can complete it.

This research has been reviewed and approved by the University of Pittsburgh's Institutional Review Board (IRB).

Statement of Consent from the Participant: I have read the above information. I do not have any questions or concerns. By continuing with the study, I indicate my consent to be in the research study.
