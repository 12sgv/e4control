Assume management is seeking input on a work policy to be implemented for your five-member team. Management values employee input and allows employees to vote for the policy they prefer. Also assume each team member’s vote will count equally, and the policy that receives more team member votes will win and therefore be implemented for that team.

Management allows you to vote for one of two policies under consideration: fully remote or fully in-office. Under the fully remote work policy, all members of your team would be required to work remotely. Under the fully in-office work policy, all members of your team would be required to be in the office during normal work hours.

Assume the policy with the most votes within your team would be implemented for your team. Which policy would you vote for?
